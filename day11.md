🚀 Day 11 of My Claude AI Learning Journey by ABTalks

What I did:
I fed my resume into an ATS optimizer against a Data Analyst job description

📄 WHAT I DID
I used Claude AI to run a full ATS analysis on my resume: gap analysis, keyword coverage, skill match scoring, and everything. 
Then I had it generate a completely optimized version with a proper UI that actually looks good enough to post.

🔍 WHAT I LEARNED:
My resume had real strengths: Python, ML projects, data dashboards but the ATS couldn't see them properly because I wasn't speaking its language.

SQL wasn't mentioned anywhere. Power BI was absent. ETL concepts existed in my projects but were buried under the wrong words. 
My Chart.js dashboards ARE interactive data dashboards

📊 THE RESULT
My current ATS score: 62%
After optimization: 84%
Industry benchmark: 70%
