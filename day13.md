🚀 Day 13 of My Claude AI Learning Journey by ABTalks

I used AI to do something most job seekers do manually for hours: I built a full Job Discovery & Analysis System using Claude AI and live job connectors.

What I did:
🔍 I fed my professional profile (AI/ML Engineer, 1+ year, Python/TensorFlow/PyTorch stack, healthcare domain) and job search criteria into Claude, then used live Indeed job connectors to pull real, active listings.
Note: Not cached results.

📊 Claude then generated a complete analysis:
• A ranked table of the top 5 opportunities with match scores and direct apply links
• The most in-demand skills across all listings (Python topped every single one)
• A gap analysis that called out my exact weaknesses: SQL, cloud platforms, MLOps, and Docker
• Market demand insights tailored to the Nigerian AI job market
• An overall fit score across 5 dimensions

What I learnt:
→ Nigeria's AI job market on Indeed is thin, but it's growing. Healthcare AI is a real differentiator.
→ My Heart Disease Prediction App is portfolio gold.
→ SQL is the fastest skill I can add to close my biggest gap.
→ Remote ML roles for Nigerian engineers live on LinkedIn and Wellfound, not Indeed.
→ Salary data is almost never disclosed on Nigerian listings. Widening my compensation lens to USD-denominated remote roles opens a much bigger door.

Learnt that that's what AI-assisted career development looks like in 2025. 
It's not replacing the job search, but making it smarter, faster, and more strategic.
