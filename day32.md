Marketing isn't a content generator.
It's a decision-making system.

That realization completely changed how I approached this project.

For Day 32 of my ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge, I challenged Claude to build something different.

Not another AI tool that spits out captions.
Not another "30 content ideas" generator.

Instead, I built Think Like a Marketing Strategist, an interactive simulator designed to teach how marketers think before they create content.

The experience walks users through the same decisions every marketer has to make:
🎯 Who are you trying to reach?
📱 Which platform actually fits that audience?
💡 What content pillars support your goals?
📅 How do you build a 30-day plan?
⚠️ And what happens when an unexpected challenge forces you to adapt?

The biggest surprise wasn't the simulator itself, it was what happened when I changed one simple thing.

Instead of letting users click through decisions, I made every screen answer one question:
"Why does this matter?"
Why this platform?
Why this audience?
Why this strategy?

That small change transformed the experience.
It no longer felt like an AI tool.
It felt like a mentor.

The biggest lesson I took away wasn't about marketing, it was about learning.
People don't learn because they're given more information.
They learn because the information is presented in the right sequence.
Audience before platform.
Platform before content.
Strategy before execution.

When the order makes sense, the "aha!" moments happen naturally.

This project reminded me that AI shouldn't just generate answers🔥.
It should help people understand how to arrive at them📌.
