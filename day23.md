🚀 Day 2 of My 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge 🟢🟠

What I did:
I took the Startup Validation Report I built earlier, and fed it straight back into Claude as a Startup Product Manager and Customer Research Expert this time. 
So instead of asking "is this a good market," I asked "what do I actually build first." 
Then Claude pulled my ICP, persona, and competitor gaps straight from the uploaded report and turned them into a Customer & MVP Blueprint: pain points, customer journey, MoSCoW prioritization, pricing hypothesis, and a Founder Action Sheet.

What I observed:
The shift from "validation" to "MVP" completely changed the kind of answers I got. 
The first report told me the market gap is real. 
This one told me to launch with exactly 3 SKUs, skip the app and the warehouse entirely, and measure success in 30 days with five concrete numbers, not vague goals like "grow the brand." 
It also scored me lower on MVP Readiness (55/100) than Customer Clarity (78/100), which forced me to admit I know my customer better than I know my supply chain right now.

What I learned:
A validation report and an MVP blueprint are two different documents doing two different jobs, and trying to make one document do both makes it weaker at each. 
Validation asks "should this exist." 
MVP planning asks "what's the smallest version that earns the right to grow." 
Feeding one AI output into the next prompt, instead of starting over, also meant nothing got lost in translation between the two😉.

Result:
I now have a clear "build this, not that" list for my brand, TeeTronics Gadgets:
3 hero SKUs, WhatsApp/IG ordering, authenticity proof, and a return policy, with everything else deliberately on the Won't-Have list for now.
