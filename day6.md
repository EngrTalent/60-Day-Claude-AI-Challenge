 Day 6 of My Claude AI Learning Journey, I Transformed a resume from an ATS Resume Optimization using Claude AI


What I did:
I started with a resume built with a basic prompt. The content was decent, but the ATS score sat at just 28/100, meaning it had a high chance of being filtered out before reaching any recruiter.

And I used Claude AI as an ATS Optimization Expert, Resume Strategist, and Recruiter, here's what changed:

📈 ATS Score Improved: 28 → 79 (+51 Points)
✅ Added a keyword-rich professional summary
✅ Standardised section headings (EXPERIENCE, SKILLS, CERTIFICATIONS)
✅ Replaced weak phrases with strong action verbs
✅ Structured skills into clean, parseable categories
✅ Fixed contact formatting to plain text


What I learnt:
Most resumes don't fail because of bad experience, they fail because ATS can't read them.

Format matters as much as content.
