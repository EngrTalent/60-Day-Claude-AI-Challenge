A denial letter feels very different when it's your story.
That realization inspired today's project.

For Day 27 of my ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge, I built something different.
Not another dashboard.
Not another workflow simulator.

But A Story.

Instead of teaching healthcare prior authorization through charts and process diagrams, I built an interactive experience that follows a patient named Rahul as he navigates diagnosis, documentation, denial, appeal, and eventually, a final decision.

What surprised me wasn't the technology.
It was the emotional shift.

The exact same facts that looked like administrative steps in yesterday's simulator suddenly felt personal when they happened to a character I had been following.

A simple "DENIED" notification wasn't just another status update anymore.
It became part of someone's journey.

That reminded me of something bigger:
The way we teach something changes the way people understand it.
A workflow helps professionals understand the process.
A story helps everyone else understand the people inside that process.
Same information.
Completely different experience.

One of my favorite lessons from today's build wasn't about AI at all.
It was about communication.
The truth of the matter is that: sometimes we don't need more information.
We just need a better way to tell the story.

Day 27/60 of my Claude AI Mastery Challenge.
