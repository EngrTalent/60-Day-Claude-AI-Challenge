Most people know what happens in a supply chain.
But very few understand why those decisions are made.
That's the challenge behind this project.

For Day 30 of my ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge, I built Supply Chain Builder, an interactive simulation that teaches beginners how real supply chains work.

So instead of throwing users into complex logistics terms, the simulator does something different.

Before every decision, it explains the trade-offs, interesting right🤩

▫️Should you manufacture closer to customers?
You'll deliver faster, but probably pay more.
▫️Should you choose the cheapest supplier?
You'll save money, but increase the risk of delays.

Every choice comes with a consequence, and every consequence comes with an explanation📌.
That completely changed how I approached prompting Claude.

I stopped asking it to build a simulator.
I started asking it to build a teacher.

And that small shift changed everything.

The biggest lesson I took away wasn't about supply chains.
It was about AI.
Good AI doesn't just give answers.
It helps people understand the reasoning behind those answers.

When AI teaches people why something works, not just what to do
It becomes much more than a productivity tool.

It becomes a learning partner.

Day 30/60 of my Claude AI Mastery Challenge.
