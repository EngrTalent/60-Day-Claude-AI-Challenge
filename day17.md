Day 17 of my 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge 🚗⛽️

What I did:
I gave Claude a 52-trip fuel dataset across 5 fuel types (Petrol, Diesel, CNG, EV, E85) and had it build a full analytics dashboard from scratch: KPI cards, SVG bar/donut/line charts, an animated scoring gauge, and a fuel-by-fuel comparison. 
Pure SVG + CSS, no charting library, no backend.

What I learned:
The data exposed something I wouldn't have guessed myself, which is that E85 is 18% cheaper at the pump than Petrol, but its mileage is ~21% lower.

Net result:
It actually costs 3.6% more per km to drive than Petrol. 
So I got to know and understand that Cheaper fuel price ≠ cheaper to run, one have to check the break-even math, and not just the pump sign alone.

The result:
A dashboard that scores E85 6.6/10 (wins big on emissions, lowest CO₂/km of all 5 fuels. But loses on real running cost), and tells me my own Petrol SUV runs ₹6.15/km, about ₹9,231/month at my usage.
