🚀 Day 7 of My Claude AI Learning Journey
 I finally stopped guessing which model to use. Instead I started being intentional about it.


What I did:
I built a system as an EEE student and daily Claude user 👇

🔵 Claude Haiku → speed tasks only. Quick definitions, brainstorming, simple lookups. Nothing more.

🟡 Claude Sonnet → my everyday workhorse. Debugging code, research summaries, exam prep, lecture notes. This handles 80% of my work.

🟣 Claude Opus → reserved for the hard stuff. Complex algorithm design, thesis-level research, multi-step engineering problems.


What I learnt:
The mistake I was making before and reaching for Max effort on everything, draining my daily quota on tasks that Standard handles just fine.

Now I have a workflow. And a workflow beats motivation every single time.
