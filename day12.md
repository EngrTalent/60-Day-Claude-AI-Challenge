What I did:
I uploaded my resume and asked Claude to run a full ATS analysis and skill gap review against a real job description: Associate Data Scientist at Reliance Health.

Here's what I learnt 👇
✅ My projects are doing the most work: 78% ATS strength. My Heart Disease Predictor App, nutrition web app, and environmental health dashboard are carrying the section that matters most.
⚠️ My skills section is where the ATS quietly filters me out, 55%. Not because the skills aren't there. Because the words aren't there. 
SQL, matplotlib, seaborn, hypothesis testing which the JD uses them. My resume doesn't. Big difference.
🚨 Overall ATS match? 61%. Competitive for a 200-level student targeting a role that asks for 2–3 years experience but not good enough yet.

The result?
3 resume fixes I can make TODAY: no new skills, just smarter wording of work I've already done. And 1 real gap I need to close urgently: SQL.

This is why I started this challenge. Not to use AI as a shortcut, but to use it as a mirror. To see what a recruiter's system actually sees when it scans my resume. 
And to close the gap between what I've built and how I'm telling the story.
