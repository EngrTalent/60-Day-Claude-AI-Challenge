I'm an Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at Landmark University, not a web developer. But today I shipped a dashboard I'm genuinely proud of.


What I did:
I gave Claude a detailed prompt and challenged it to build something real:

🌍 Personal Environmental Health Analyzer 👉 Nigeria Edition
A fully interactive dashboard that:

📊 Pulls real AQI & PM2.5/PM10 data for 8 Nigerian cities
💧 Analyzes water quality and its impact on hair and skin health
 📈 Visualizes city-by-city rankings with live, filterable charts 
📋 Generates an Environmental Health Report Card with A–F grades 
🚦 Maps health risks across lungs, sleep, energy, and exercise performance 
💡 Delivers personalized daily recommendations per city


What I learnt:
Prompting isn't about asking AI to do things for you. It's about thinking clearly enough to describe a system, and letting AI engineer it.

I didn't touch a code editor. No Stack Overflow. No debugging sessions. No framework setup.
Just one well-structured prompt, and Claude architected the entire thing. Particle animations, Chart.js visualizations, tabbed navigation, interactive city cards, risk scoring logic, the full design system.
