🚀 Day 22 of My 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge 🟢🟠

What I did:
I turned Claude into a Startup Advisor, VC Analyst, and Market Research Expert using just one prompt.
I used it to validate TeeTronics Gadgets, my own tech retail brand, the way an investor actually would. 
And I fed it with my idea, the problem I'm solving, my target customers, and where I am right now (pre-revenue, SMEDAN-registered, zero sales yet). 
Then I asked for the full works: TAM/SAM/SOM, founder-market fit score, competitor analysis, ICP, risk assessment, and a 30-day action plan built as a consulting-style report with charts, not just text.

What I observed:
Claude didn't just flatter the idea back to me ...amazing, right? 
It pulled current market data on Nigeria's electronics and e-commerce space, mapped out exactly where Jumia, Slot, Pointek, and the informal Computer Village market sit on price and trust, and then scored my own founder-fit at 6.8/10
It's strong on execution and resilience, honest about my weakest spot being capital access🥲.
It even built a TAM → SAM → SOM funnel chart and a risk matrix in my brand colors without me asking twice😌.

What I learned:
A real validation report doesn't start with "yes, great idea." 
It starts with "here's the market-level evidence for the problem, now show me YOUR evidence." 
Claude's verdict was a Conditional Go: the gap is real, but I haven't tested anything with actual customers yet. 
That distinction between "the market problem is validated" and "my business is validated" is one I hadn't been making clearly enough before this.

The result:
I now have a full PDF-ready Startup Validation Report for TeeTronics Gadgets, and more importantly, a concrete 30-day plan to go run real customer conversations and a pilot sale before I spend a single naira on inventory. 
Sometimes the most valuable thing an AI tool can tell a founder isn't "go," it's "go, but here's what you still need to prove first."
