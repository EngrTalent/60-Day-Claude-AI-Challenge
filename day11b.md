🚀 Day 11 of My Claude AI Learning Journey by ABTalks
I ran my resume through an ATS optimizer for a Data Scientist role at M-KOPA, and it taught me something I wasn't expecting. 🧵

What I did:
I used Claude AI to run a full ATS gap analysis on my resume against a real Data Scientist job description: M-KOPA, one of Africa's fastest-growing fintech companies and a TIME 100 Most Influential company.


What I learnt:
My ML work was real. My projects were solid. But my resume wasn't saying it right.

→ scikit-learn, pandas, and numpy weren't mentioned anywhere, even though I used all three in my Heart Disease Predictor. ATS filters skipped right past my project.
→ "Trained and evaluated a model" is weak. The JD wanted feature engineering, hyperparameter tuning, cross-validation, model validation, and deployment. Trust me I did all. I just never said so.
→ My Environmental Health Analyzer covered 8 Nigerian cities with real-time API data. For a company operating across 5 African markets, that's a mission alignment signal, but only if you frame it that way.
→ Translating complex model outputs for non-technical audiences? I do that every day through ABTalks. I just wasn't calling it "stakeholder communication."

The gap wasn't in my skills. It was in my vocabulary.


📊 THE RESULT
✅ ATS score: 71% → 91%
✅ All critical gaps fixed: libraries named, ML pipeline reframed, Africa angle led
✅ Resume fully redesigned: ATS score badge, skill bars, project cards, two-column layout
