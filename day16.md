🚀 Day 16 of My Claude AI Learning Journey by ABTalks

I put three of India's biggest names side by side: TCS, Tata Motors, and HDFC Bank.

What I did:
I ran a full fundamentals comparison across one IT services giant, one auto manufacturer with global exposure (Tata Motors' passenger vehicle arm owns Jaguar Land Rover), and one of India's largest private banks. 
I pulled live data on valuation, growth, balance sheet strength, ownership trends, and risk for each.

What I found:
→ TCS is sitting at a P/E of ~16x, well below its own 10-year average of ~26x, despite still posting a 52% ROE and a debt-free balance sheet. 
The stock looks statistically cheap relative to its own history, even though revenue growth has slowed.

→ Tata Motors just went through a major corporate event: it demerged into two separate listed companies in October 2025, splitting commercial vehicles from passenger vehicles + EVs + JLR. 
Combine that with a cyberattack that disrupted JLR's production this year, and its numbers are genuinely the noisiest of the three right now.

→ HDFC Bank quietly has the strongest 5-year revenue growth engine of the group (22% CAGR) and the clearest sign of conviction buying, domestic institutions have grown their stake from 27% to over 40% in three years, more than offsetting steady foreign selling.

What stood out comparing all three:
All three stocks are trading well below their 52-week highs at the same time, but for completely different reasons, a global IT spending slowdown for TCS, a one-off operational shock for Tata Motors, and a post-merger "digestion" period for HDFC Bank. 
Same direction, three very different stories underneath.

What I learned:
Headline numbers like P/E and ROE can mislead one if one don't ask why they look the way they do. 
Tata Motors' P/E looked oddly distorted until I traced it to a one-off accounting gain. 
HDFC Bank's falling ROE looked like weakness until I connected it to the HDFC Ltd merger diluting the equity base. 
Context turns raw numbers into an actual read on a business.
