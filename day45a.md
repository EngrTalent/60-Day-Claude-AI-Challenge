Forty-five days ago, I challenged myself to build one AI-powered project every single day☺️.

You know I thought I was learning Claude, but looking back, I was really learning how to build💯.

For the past 45 days, I've been documenting my journey through the ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge, shipping one working project every day and sharing every lesson publicly.
Not mockups, not ideas, but working applications🫴.

From healthcare simulators and supply chain decision engines to prompt engineering trainers, productivity tools, and AI-powered learning experiences, every project pushed me to think a little differently than the day before.
The biggest surprise wasn't how much better the projects became, it was how much better my process became✨.

In the beginning, I focused on getting the app to work.
Now, I spend more time asking the right questions before writing a single prompt.
Who is this for?
What problem does it solve?
How should someone experience it?

That shift changed everything, and the projects became more intentional.
The prompts became clearer, and the lessons became bigger than the code itself🙌.

If there's one thing these 45 days have taught me, it's this:
Consistency compounds💯.
One finished project teaches more than five unfinished ideas📌.
One thoughtful reflection teaches more than another tutorial🔥.
And one habit repeated daily can quietly reshape the way you think✨.

Today, I can look back and see something that didn't exist six weeks ago.
Not just 45 completed projects✨.
But a body of work💯.
A repeatable process✨.
A stronger understanding of product design, prompt engineering, and AI-assisted development🔥.

Most importantly, I've learned that building in public isn't about proving what you know, it's about documenting how you're growing📌.

If I had to choose one project that represents this journey best, it would be PromptCoach.
Not because it's the flashiest build, but because it reflects how my thinking evolved🔥.
It doesn't just use AI, it teaches people how to communicate with AI more effectively📌.
And that's a skill I believe will matter for years to come💯.

With 15 days still to go, I'm excited to see how much further this challenge can take me😉.
Day 45/60.
Still learning, still building, and still sharing the journey.
