🚀 Day 14 of My Claude AI Learning Journey by ABTalks

What I did:
I pasted a job description into Claude AI and asked it to act as a Red Flag Detector.

The result
A full risk report scored, categorized, and broken down 

Here's what it flagged on this "Junior Full Stack Developer" listing:

🚩 "Junior" role requiring 5+ years experience (score: 10/10)
🚩 "Remote" job with hidden relocation clause
🚩 No salary disclosed at an 8-month-old unfunded startup
🚩 24/7 on-call support expected with no mention of compensation
🚩 A tech stack that requires React + Angular + Vue + Node + Python + Java + Go + 3 cloud platforms for just one person

Overall Risk Score: 88/100 which clearly means I should AVOID.

What I learnt building this:

Claude isn't just a chatbot. When you give it a structured role and a clear output format, it becomes a thinking tool. 
Job hunting is exhausting. AI can help you protect yourself while you search.
