Guess what, I had AI roast my LinkedIn profile today, and it wasn't kind at all🥲
But it was exactly what I needed to be honest.

For Day 44 of my ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge, I decided to turn the lens on myself instead of building something for someone else😉.

I ran a complete audit of my LinkedIn profile: my headline, About section, experience, skills, and overall positioning to see whether my profile actually reflected the work I've been doing.

The result surprised me.
My profile scored 38/100.

Not because it contained false information, but because so much of the story was missing😪.
No About section.
No listed skills.
Experience entries that described what I did instead of the impact I created.

The biggest surprise wasn't the score, it was realizing how easy it is to confuse information with positioning.

A headline packed with titles doesn't tell people who you are📌.
An empty About section doesn't just leave space on your profile, it leaves questions in the minds of anyone who visits it📌.

So I rewrote everything with one goal in mind:
Not to make my profile sound more impressive, but to make it communicate more clearly✨.

By the end of the session, my profile score had improved from 38/100 to 74/100, along with a practical seven-day plan to turn a stronger profile into greater visibility🔥.

The biggest lesson I took away from today wasn't about LinkedIn, it was about personal branding.

Your experience speaks for itself only after people understand it📌.

Before that, your profile is doing all the talking.

And if your profile is only listing titles instead of telling your story, it may be working against you without you even realizing it✨.

Day 44/60 of my Claude AI Mastery Challenge.
