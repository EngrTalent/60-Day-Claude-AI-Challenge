🚀 Day 9 of My Claude AI Learning Journey
What I did:
Built something I'm genuinely proud of: NutriScope Pro, a precision nutrition intelligence web app, 100% frontend, zero backend. 🔬


⚡ What NutriScope Pro does:
✅ Profiles your body scientifically: Uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation to calculate your BMR, TDEE, and BMI. Then sets personalised macro and micronutrient targets based on your age, gender, height, weight, activity level, and health goal (maintain / lose / gain).

✅ Tracks 15 nutrients across 60 foods Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat, Fiber, Iron, Calcium, Vitamins C/D/B12, Zinc, Magnesium, Potassium, Vitamin A, and Folate. All from a curated Indian-focused food database (Rice, Dal, Paneer, Spinach, Fish, Moong Dal, Chia Seeds, Flaxseed, and 52 more).

✅ CSV Upload + Live Food Log Drag & drop a CSV to bulk-import meals instantly. Edit quantities inline. Everything persists in localStorage, no server needed.

✅ 6 interactive Chart.js visualisations Energy balance ring · Macro doughnut · Micronutrient radar · 7-day calorie trend · Micronutrient % bar chart · Risk score chart. All dark-themed and responsive.

✅ Risk Analysis engine Flags up to 9 nutritional risk categories: Anaemia, Bone Density, Neurological (B12), Vitamin D deficiency, Metabolic risk, and more, each with a Low / Med / High severity score and science-backed explanation.

✅ 2-Day Meal Planner Plan Breakfast, Lunch, and Dinner across two days with live nutrition totals per day which is separate from your daily log.

✅ Smart Recommendations + Food Swaps + Portion Guidance Personalised to your dietary preference (Vegetarian / Non-Veg / Eggetarian), health goal, and actual deficiencies. Not generic tips, it's specific, actionable suggestions like "Swap White Rice → Brown Rice" with the nutritional reason.

✅ Sources & Educational Disclaimer References ICMR-NIN 2020, USDA FoodData Central, WHO DRI guidelines, and Harvard T.H. Chan SPH.


This was built as a single HTML file, no frameworks, no npm, no backend. Just clean HTML, CSS, and JavaScript working together.
