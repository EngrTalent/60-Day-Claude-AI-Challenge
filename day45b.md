I thought capital was the biggest thing holding me back, but guess what ...I was solving the wrong problem.

For Day 45 of my ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge, I used Claude to run a decision audit on launching TeeTronics Gadgets.

Instead of asking, "Should I launch?" I explored three different paths:
Launch immediately at resumption.
Delay until I had enough capital.
Or start with a soft launch before resumption.

Each option was scored across seven factors, from opportunity and financial risk to stress, confidence, and long-term regret.
I expected the safest option to be delaying the launch, but it wasn't.

The biggest surprise was realizing that the real uncertainty wasn't my budget, it was my supply chain.
I had spent so much time thinking about raising enough capital that I'd overlooked a much simpler question:
Can I actually trust my vendor?
That single question changed everything.
A soft launch suddenly made far more sense than waiting indefinitely.

Start with a small range of accessories, test one vendor, validate the process, learn from real customers, and then scale with confidence.

The biggest lesson I took away from today's project wasn't about funding, it was about entrepreneurship.
Sometimes the obstacle you're trying to solve isn't the one that's actually slowing you down.
For me, the real risk wasn't launching with less money, it was launching without enough evidence that my supply chain could deliver.

And this project didn't just give me a recommendation, it gave me clarity.

TeeTronics Gadgets now has a practical seven-day action plan instead of an open-ended "someday."

Day 45/60 of my Claude AI Mastery Challenge.
