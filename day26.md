Could AI build a realistic professional training simulator from a single prompt?

I decided to find out.

For Day 26 of my ABTalks 60-Day Claude AI Mastery Challenge, I challenged Claude to recreate one of the most frustrating administrative processes in healthcare:
Prior Authorization.

Instead of generating another report or dashboard, I asked it to build an interactive simulator.

The result?
A complete workflow where you step into the roles of a patient, provider, and insurance payer, moving cases from request to final decision.

As I tested different scenarios, one pattern became obvious.
Cases with weak documentation kept getting denied.
Cases with stronger evidence consistently moved toward approval.

That changed how I think about the process.

It's easy to assume prior authorization is just bureaucracy.
But underneath it is a much simpler question:
"Have you provided enough evidence to justify this decision?"
That insight applies far beyond healthcare.

Whether you're pitching investors, applying for grants, requesting approval, or defending a project, the quality of your evidence often matters more than the volume of your paperwork.
The most exciting part for me wasn't the healthcare lesson, though.
It was realizing that Claude helped me ship an interactive educational tool in a single HTML file, with no backend, no frameworks, and no external dependencies.

Every day of this challenge reinforces the same idea:
AI isn't replacing creativity.
It's dramatically reducing the time between an idea and a working prototype.

Day 26/60 of my Claude AI Mastery Challenge.
