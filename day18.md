🚀 Day 18 of My Claude AI Learning Journey by ABTalks

I turned a messy meeting transcript into a decision-ready dashboard, with just one prompt 🟢🟧

What I did:
I took a raw 7-speaker quarterly strategy meeting transcript and had Claude restructure it into a full interactive dashboard: decisions, action items with owners and deadlines, risks, conflicts, and open questions, broken down by speaker.

What I learned:
Claude doesn't just summarize, it attributes who decided what, flags conflicts that aren't obvious on a first read (like two teams competing for the same engineering capacity), and explicitly tells you what's not yet decided instead of guessing. That last part matters more than the summary itself.

What I observed:
Real meetings are messy. For instance, owners aren't always named, deadlines are vague ("next month," "next week"), and the same risk gets mentioned three different ways by three different people. And guess what, Claude stayed honest about every gap instead of just filling it in.

The result:
A clean, shareable dashboard built from raw, unstructured notes in minutes, the kind of artifact a PM or exec assistant could hand straight to leadership.
